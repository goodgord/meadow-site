Help with Meadow.

Using Meadow

  • Press play to start. The music begins quietly and grows.
  • Swipe across the garden to change piece. There are six.
  • Hold keeps the current chord ringing. Press it again to let the music move on.
  • Next moves to a new chord.
  • Regrow gives the bells new patterns.
  • Controls lets you change the layers, tone, pulse, space and scale of a piece. Each piece keeps its own settings.
  • To put a piece back the way it was, open Controls, go to Setup and choose Reset.
  • The music keeps playing when your phone is locked. You can pause it or change piece from the lock screen.

Contact

If something isn’t working, or you have a question, please open an issue on GitHub.
