Meadow

Slow ambient music for iPhone.

Meadow plays music that grows and changes by itself. Soft chords drift underneath. Bells ring in long loops that never quite line up, so the music keeps moving without repeating.

Each piece is drawn as a small garden. Every stem is a loop of bells, and its bud glows when a bell sounds.

Put it on while you read, work or fall asleep. It keeps playing when your phone is locked.

There are six pieces, each with its own scale and mood. Read about them.

Meadow is free. It has no ads, no accounts and no tracking.
